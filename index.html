Our Story

Made by us.
For all of us.

There is a pattern that has played out for too long. Someone from outside comes to Kenya, sees something extraordinary about us, our humour, our languages, our warmth, our way of looking at the world, and either takes it, or is the first to tell us it is special.

Kenyan Feels was created to disrupt that pattern. We name what is ours. We design it. We celebrate it.

These mugs carry messages shaped by our worldview, not imported positivity or borrowed narratives. Real encouragement, real humour, real love. The kind that sounds like home.

Our Languages

Kenya's 42+ ethnic languages are living heritage. Our designs are one small way to keep them visible and valued.

Our Messages

Positive, grounding, and aligned with how we actually see the world. Not borrowed from elsewhere.

Our Diaspora

For those living abroad: a tangible piece of home that sits on your desk every morning.

Our Standard

Designed in Nairobi. Every purchase supports a Kenyan creative ecosystem we are building together.

This is not just about mugs. It is about reclaiming the right to define ourselves, and making that definition beautiful enough to drink from every day.

Anne

Founder & Creative Director, Kenyan Feels · Nairobi
